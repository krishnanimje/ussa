## 1. Ethical Link Building Philosophy

Search engines, specifically Google's SpamBrain and link evaluation systems, penalize artificial link schemes, automated directory blasts, and paid backlink networks.

### Ground Rules & Non-Negotiables:
1. **Zero Link Schemes:** No Fiverr backlink packs, no private blog networks (PBNs), no link exchange rings, and no automated forum comment spam.
2. **Relevance Over Raw Volume:** One contextual link from an authoritative San Diego publication or military transition resource provides exponentially more ranking value than 500 low-quality offshore directories.
3. **Strict Compliance:** Adherence to Google Search Essentials and California DRE marketing and advertising disclosure mandates (every public profile must clearly display DRE #02375273 and Full Circle Group).
