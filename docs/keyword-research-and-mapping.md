## 3. Search Intent Analysis & Content Architecture

### 1. High-Intent Commercial Searches
- **Query Structure:** `[Neighborhood / Specialty] + "homes for sale"` or `"Realtor"`.
- **User Pain Point:** Needing accurate MLS pricing, knowing whether properties qualify for specific financing (VA, FHA, Conventional), avoiding unvetted third-party aggregate lead capture.
- **On-Page Implementation:** Fast-loading server-rendered listings with verified beds, baths, price per square foot, HOA clarity, and instant tour booking.

### 2. VA Military Relocation Searches
- **Query Structure:** `"VA loan San Diego 0 down"`, `"Camp Pendleton housing BAH purchase"`.
- **User Pain Point:** Sellers mistakenly rejecting VA offers due to fear of appraisal delays; confusing PCS timeline with escrow closing.
- **On-Page Implementation:** Clear educational breakdowns of the Blue Water Navy Vietnam Veterans Act (elimination of VA loan limits), verified VA eligibility badges on listings, and BAH purchasing power calculator integration.

### 3. Hispanic Community & Bilingual Searches
- **Query Structure:** `"Agente de bienes raíces en San Diego que hable español"`, `"comprar casa primer comprador"`.
- **User Pain Point:** Linguistic barrier in legal escrow contracts, fear of predatory lending, misinformation regarding the mandatory 20% down payment.
- **On-Page Implementation:** 100% native Spanish landing pages with bidirectional `hreflang` tags, explaining FHA 3.5% down, CalHFA subsidies, and step-by-step escrow transparency.

## 4. Anti-Cannibalization Guidelines

1. **One Primary Target Keyword Per URL:** Distinct search queries must point to distinct canonical pages. Never optimize multiple landing pages for identical head terms.
2. **Descriptive URL Slugs:** Always reflect the primary keyword in the path (e.g., `/va-loans`, `/first-time-homebuyer`, `/neighborhoods/north-park`).
3. **Internal Anchor Text Precision:** When internally linking from blog posts or neighborhood pages, use target keyword anchor text rather than generic "click here" or "read more".
